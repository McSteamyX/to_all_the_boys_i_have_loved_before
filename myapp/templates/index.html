About The Book

Young Adult Romance Contemporary Fiction

Lara Jean Song has a secret: whenever she falls in love, she writes a letter to the boy, expressing every feeling she could never say out loud. These letters are personal, honest and never intended to be sent. Safely tucked away in a hatbox, they represent the different chapters of her romantic life.

But when the letters unexpectedly find their way to the boys they were written for, Lara Jean's quiet world becomes anything but ordinary. Suddenly, she must navigate unexpected attention, complicated feelings and relationships she never planned for. Jenny Han delivers a delightful story about growing up, discovering love and learning to embrace life's surprises.

01
Chapter One: The Letters I Never Sent

I have always believed that some things are better left unsaid.

Not because they aren't important, but because sometimes putting feelings into words makes them too real. And sometimes, the thought of someone knowing exactly how you feel is far more terrifying than keeping it to yourself.

For me, those feelings have always belonged on paper. There is something comforting about writing a letter to someone you will never send it to. You can say everything. You can be honest, dramatic, embarrassing, even a little ridiculous. Nobody interrupts you. Nobody judges you. And most importantly, nobody ever has to know.

At least, that was the plan.

I used to think that the hardest part of falling for someone was admitting it to yourself. I was wrong. The hardest part is discovering what happens when a secret you have carefully protected suddenly stops being a secret.

I never imagined that a few handwritten letters could turn my perfectly ordinary life into something I couldn't control.

But then again, I had never imagined that the boys I had written to would actually read them.

And that was where everything began.

About the Author

Jenny Han

New York Times Bestselling Author | Writer | Producer

Jenny Han is a bestselling American author celebrated for her heartwarming young adult novels exploring love, friendship, family and the journey of growing up.
She is best known for the To All the Boys I've Loved Before trilogy and The Summer I Turned Pretty series, both of which have been adapted into popular screen productions.

With her engaging storytelling, memorable characters and ability to capture the complexities of teenage life, Jenny has built a special connection with readers around the world. Her books have been translated into more than thirty languages, introducing her stories to audiences across different cultures.

Beyond writing, Jenny has also worked as a television creator, showrunner and executive producer, bringing many of her beloved fictional worlds to the screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Curious about Jenny Han, her books or where to begin? Find your answers here

Who is Jenny Han?

Jenny Han is an American author known for her young adult fiction novels. She is the author of the "To All the Boys I've Loved Before" series, which has been adapted into a successful film franchise.

What genre is To All the Boys I've Loved Before?

It is a young adult contemporary romance novel with coming-of-age themes, exploring first love, family, friendship and growing up.

How many books are in the To All the Boys series?

There are four books in the To All the Boys series: "To All the Boys I've Loved Before," "P.S. I Still Love You," and "Always and Forever, Lara Jean"

What other books has Jenny Han written?

Some of her other works include The Summer I Turned Pretty trilogy, Shug, Clara Lee and the Apple Pie Dream and the Burn for Burn trilogy, co-written with Siobhan Vivian.

Is To All the Boys I've Loved Before a movie?

Yes! The novel was adapted into a Netflix film trilogy starring Lana Condor and Noah Centineo.

In what order should I read the To All the Boys books?

Start with To All the Boys I've Loved Before, continue with P.S. I Still Love You and finish with Always and Forever, Lara Jean.
